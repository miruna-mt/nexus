📋 The scenario

A consumer electronics brand has €1,000,000 to invest across its annual marketing budget. The CMO's team must decide how to split it across 6 strategic investment blocks — from performance media to creative production.

🎯 Optimization goal

Maximize risk-adjusted ROI — balancing high-return, high-variance blocks against stable performers.

📊 Key numbers

  • 6 investment blocks: Performance Media, Brand Media, Content & SEO, Web & CRO, PR & Events, Creative Production
  • €1,000,000 annual marketing budget
  • Target: Adults 30-50 · Urban · Mid-to-high income

💡 Why it matters

Same Markowitz engine used for financial portfolios, now applied to marketing investment. Diversification across blocks reduces planning risk without sacrificing expected return.

📐 Industry framework (70-20-10)

The model's output maps naturally to the standard marketing investment framework:

  • 70% Core / Performance → Performance Media, Web & CRO
  • 20% Growth / Brand → Brand Media, Content & SEO
  • 10% Experimentation → PR & Events, Creative Production
