📋 The scenario

An armoured cash operator runs 4 trucks out of a Madrid depot, serving 12 bank branches, 8 ATMs and 4 retail cash points across the city.

🎯 Optimization goal

Find the shortest set of routes that covers all 24 stops while respecting tight morning time windows and vehicle capacity.

📊 Key numbers

  • 4 armoured trucks · 500 units each
  • 24 stops across Madrid · 1,875 units of demand
  • Real locations: banks, ATMs and retail across the city

💡 Why it matters

Cash-in-transit is high-risk, regulated and time-sensitive. The same routing engine that moves fresh produce moves cash.
