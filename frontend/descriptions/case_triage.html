📋 The scenario

A mid-size bank has 200 pending investigation cases across 6 specialized teams: Accounts, Cards, Fraud, VIP, Compliance and General Complaints.

🎯 Optimization goal

Distribute the backlog so the busiest team is as unloaded as possible — without exceeding each team's weekly capacity.

📊 Key numbers

  • 200 cases · 6 specialized teams
  • 1,029 hours of work · 240 hours/day of capacity
  • Priority-weighted: high, medium and low

💡 Why it matters

Banks receive thousands of complaints and alerts daily. Most are resolved at first contact. The remainder becomes this backlog — and clearing it is a real operational decision.
