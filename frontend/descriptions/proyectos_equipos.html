📋 The scenario

A digital marketing agency must distribute 12 live projects across 5 specialized teams — from Integrated Campaign to CRM & Lifecycle. Each team has a fixed number of members and a maximum weekly capacity.

🎯 Optimization goal

Minimize the busiest team's backlog by balancing workload across teams, without exceeding each team's capacity.

📊 Key numbers

  • 12 projects · 5 specialized teams
  • 1,555 hours of work · 780 hours/day of total capacity
  • Priority-weighted: high, medium and low

💡 Why it matters

Same workload balancing engine that distributes bank cases, now applied to agency team capacity. The model finds the assignment that keeps the busiest team as unloaded as possible — a real operational decision.
