📋 The scenario

An agricultural cooperative delivers fresh produce to 14 destinations across Spain.

🎯 Optimization goal

Assign each stop to one of 4 trucks and sequence the visits to minimize total distance.

📊 Key numbers

  • 4 trucks · 150 boxes each
  • 14 real destinations · Murcia, Madrid, Barcelona
  • Real distances (Haversine over GPS coordinates)

💡 Why it matters

The Vehicle Routing Problem — the operational core of every distributor, cooperative, and food chain.
