📋 The scenario

A luxury watch brand wants to reach adults 30-60, urban, high income. Its agency must decide how to split a €2,000,000 media budget across 7 channels — from TV and OTT to digital, print, outdoor and radio.

🎯 Optimization goal

Maximize ROAS delivered to the target audience across 7 media — at controlled risk.

📊 Key numbers

  • 7 media channels: TV/OTT, Print, Social Paid, Digital Search, Digital Display, OOH, Radio
  • €2,000,000 media budget
  • Target: Adults 30-60 · Urban · High income

💡 Why it matters

Media planning is strategic work: matching a brand to where its audience actually lives. Markowitz applied here means diversification across channels, which reduces reach risk without sacrificing delivery.
