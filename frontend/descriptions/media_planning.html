📋 The scenario

A luxury watch brand wants to reach adults 25-59, urban, high income. Its agency has mapped where this target moves across the Spanish TV landscape and must decide how to allocate the media budget.

🎯 Optimization goal

Maximize GRP delivered to the target audience across 12 network × daypart combinations — at controlled risk.

📊 Key numbers

  • 12 network × daypart combinations
  • €500,000 media budget
  • Target: Adults 25-59 · Urban · High income

💡 Why it matters

Media planning is strategic work: matching a brand to where its audience actually lives. Markowitz applied here means diversification across networks, which reduces reach risk without sacrificing delivery.
