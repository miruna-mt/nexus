📋 The scenario

A fund must split €100,000 across 5 assets under regulatory caps.

🎯 Optimization goal

Find the weight of each asset that maximizes risk-adjusted return.

📊 Key numbers

  • 5 assets · €100,000 capital
  • Risk aversion adjustable 0–10
  • Per-asset regulatory caps

💡 Why it matters

The Markowitz model (Nobel Prize, 1990) — the foundation of every modern portfolio.
