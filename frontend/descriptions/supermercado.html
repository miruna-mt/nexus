📋 The scenario

A supermarket chain plans weekly orders for 6 perishable products over 12 weeks.

🎯 Optimization goal

Minimize total cost (ordering + storage + out-of-stock) while meeting demand.

📊 Key numbers

  • 6 perishables · 12-week horizon
  • €50/order · €0.05 stored/unit/week · €2 out-of-stock
  • Warehouse capacity: 3,000 units

💡 Why it matters

The Economic Order Quantity model (Harris, 1913) — typical savings of 30–50% on inventory costs.
